# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Experienced traders and investors in Indonesia who already have market knowledge. They use the platform as a professional intelligence tool, not to learn the basics. Context: high-stakes decision-making, time-sensitive, often on desktop during market hours.

## Product Purpose

Directional Outlook & Macro Alpha Intelligence (DOMAIN). A market radar powered by AI and professionals, delivering unfiltered news with impact analysis, trading plans, 1v1 consultation, and education. Success: operators trust it as their second-opinion copilot for blind-spot coverage.

## Brand Personality

Tactical, uncompromising, classified. Military/intelligence aesthetic. The product gatekeeps intentionally: onboarding is a filter, not a funnel.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with rounded cards and pastel gradients
- Discord/community-style chat platforms
- Beginner-friendly fintech with gamification (Robinhood, eToro)
- Crypto projects with neon-on-black cliches
- Any design that looks "friendly" or "approachable"

## Design Principles

1. **Briefing, not browsing.** Every screen should feel like receiving classified intelligence, not scrolling a feed.
2. **Earned access.** The UI reinforces exclusivity. No hand-holding, no onboarding tooltips.
3. **Signal over decoration.** Typography and layout do the work. Ornament is minimal and functional.
4. **Tension through restraint.** Dark backgrounds, monospace type, yellow (#FFD400) as the sole accent. Let negative space create weight.
5. **Show competence.** The interface assumes the user is skilled. Dense information is a feature, not a problem.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast ratios. Reduced motion support via prefers-reduced-motion. Keyboard navigable. No reliance on color alone for status communication.
