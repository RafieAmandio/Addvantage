| Attribute | Detail |

| **Product Surface** | DOMAIN — Directional Outlook & Macro Alpha Intelligence |
| **Concept** | Market radar + copilot powered by AI and professional traders |

ANTS is **not** a research subscription, a signals group, or a trading school. It is a market radar — a one-way intelligence stream meant to sharpen the edge of people who already trade. Distribution is Telegram-first because that is where the audience already lives; the web app exists to host the surfaces that Telegram cannot (gated trading plan, 1v1 consultation, education library).

## 2. Positioning

### What is this?

> A market radar powered by AI and professionals, created for traders and investors who already know what they're doing.

### Who is this *not* for?

- Beginners
- Gamblers
- Hobbyists

We do not soften this. The product is built around the assumption that the user already understands risk, position sizing, and market structure. Onboarding should filter, not convert.

### Who is this actually for?

- Experienced market participants trying to level up their edge
- Traders who need a market copilot for second-opinion / blind-spot coverage
- Anyone who wants to activate "**Six Eyes**" on the market — the feeling of having continuous, multi-angle situational awareness

## 4. Trading Psychology Layer

A first-class part of the product, not a blog category. The goal is to give traders shared language for the failure modes they all hit.

- **Named theory primers** — short explainers built around real frameworks (e.g. *Kahneman's Loss Aversion*, prospect theory, recency bias, sunk-cost trap).
- **Hashtag system for common problems** — every recurring trader pathology gets a tag that runs across posts, education, and consultation:
  - `#losing-streak` — process for surviving and exiting drawdown
  - `#unprofitability` — diagnosing why an edge isn't there yet
  - `#developing-edge` — building, testing, and trusting a new system
  - (more to be added as patterns emerge)
- The hashtag system is the connective tissue between News, Trading Plan, Education, and Consultation — a user reading about a losing streak in education can pull every consultation log, post, and primer with the same tag.

## 6. Distribution: Telegram-First Funnel

Telegram is **the** funnel. Every other surface — web, streams, social — exists to push users into Telegram, and Telegram pushes them into the paid web product.

- Telegram is where free pillars (news, calendar, my channel, psychology tags) are delivered.
- Telegram is where streams happen (or are mirrored) for conversion.
- Telegram is where the call-to-action to upgrade lives.
- The web app (DOMAIN dashboard) is the destination for paid users only — Trading Plan, 1v1 Consultation, full education.

This means the web product can be smaller and sharper than a typical SaaS — it does not need to handle discovery, onboarding noise, or community.

---

## 7. Influencer / Team Collab Channels

ANTS is designed to host guest voices without losing the one-way broadcast model.

- An external influencer or partner trader can be given a **dedicated channel** inside the system to share their info / trades / takes.
- Each collab channel is its own broadcast surface — still one-way, still no chat, still tagged into the same hashtag system.
- This lets ANTS scale voice without scaling moderation, and gives partners a clean place to publish.

---

## 8. Anti-Community Philosophy

We are explicit and proud about *not* having a community / chat / forum. This is a feature, not an omission. The FAQ on the landing page should answer the inevitable question head-on:

> **"Will there be a community / chat?"**
>
> Not for now. Four reasons:
>
> 1. **Noise reduction.** No herd mentality, no group-think distorting your read on the market.
> 2. **No paid moderators.** AI moderation is still imperfect, and we refuse to charge users for a chat we cannot reliably regulate.
> 3. **Emotional contagion.** Other people's panic and euphoria are the single biggest leak in most traders' P&L. We will not pipe that into your screen.
> 4. **Privacy & intellectual property.** Trade discussions belong in closed 1v1 consultation, not in a public room where your edge becomes everyone's edge.

---

## 9. Liability Agreement

At the end of the signup / upgrade flow, every user must sign a liability agreement that explicitly releases ANTS from responsibility for any trading losses incurred from acting on platform content. This is non-optional. No agreement, no access.
